# Sockpuppet & OPSEC

Investigation leaks in both directions: your target can see you back. This
covers protecting the investigator and running research personas — for lawful,
authorized work only.

## Protect yourself first

- **Separate environment** — a dedicated browser profile or VM for research;
  never mix with personal accounts. Containers/multi-account browsers isolate
  cookies.
- **Network** — a VPN or the Tor Browser hides your IP and rough location from
  sites that log visitors. Assume every page you load records you.
- **No live interaction from real accounts** — viewing a LinkedIn profile logged
  in tips off the target; viewing a Story marks you as a viewer. Observe from a
  research account or logged out.
- **Disable link previews** in the tools you paste target URLs into — they fetch
  the URL and can leak to the target.

## Research personas (sockpuppets)

When observation requires an account:

- Build a **plausible, consistent** persona (name, photo, history) that fits the
  platform. AI-generated faces avoid reverse-image hits — verify they don't.
- Age it: a brand-new empty account is a red flag and gets banned. Establish
  normal activity before use.
- **Compartmentalize** — a unique email and phone per persona; never cross-link
  personas or touch them from your real identity.
- Match the persona's apparent locale (language, timezone) to its backstory.

## Rules

- Sockpuppets are for **observation**, not for deceiving people into private
  info, entrapment, or accessing systems — that crosses legal and ethical lines.
- Many platforms' ToS forbid fake accounts; weigh that against your mandate.

See [../../ETHICS.md](../../ETHICS.md). If you can't justify the persona, don't
create it.
